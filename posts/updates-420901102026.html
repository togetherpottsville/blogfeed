St Marks Anglican Church

ppine76@gmail.com 

  • Blessing of the Pets: Sunday 25 October at 10:30am at St Mark’s Pottsville in the Community Garden.
  • Christmas Festive Market: 28 November at St James’ Anglican Church, Marine Parade, Kingscliff from 8:00am. A joint event with St Mark’s Pottsville. Cakes, puddings, jams & pickles, plants, used clothing, craft, men’s tools, BBQ, barista coffee, information tent, face painting and lucky dips for the kids. Pottsville Beach Public School P&C Association

pandc.pottsville@gmail.com 

We are Pottsville Beach Public School P&C ASSOCIATION! We connect with families, strengthen our school community, and support important school initiatives through fundraising and volunteering.

  • Open positions: We currently have open executive positions for a President, Treasurer, and Fundraising Coordinator and encourage anyone interested to get involved. Parents, grandparents, relatives, friends and community members are all welcome. Every contribution helps strengthen our P&C and support our school. If you’d like to learn more about a role, please contact us at pandc.pottsville@gmail.com 
  • Donations: If you’d like to support our school in providing essential resources and equipment for our amazing students, you can make a tax-deductible donation via GIVENOW. Every contribution, big or small, makes a real difference.
  • Raffle prizes!: Starting on the 1st October, and in collaboration with Pottsville Beach Sports Club we will be holding a raffle! Prizes include a $500 Foodworks voucher, vouchers to Crystal Castle and more to come! Raffles are $5 / ticket or $10 for 3 and can be purchased at the school office or at the bowls club.
